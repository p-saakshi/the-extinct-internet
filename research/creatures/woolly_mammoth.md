# Woolly Mammoth — Quick Research Notes

**Scientific name:** *Mammuthus primigenius*  
**Rank:** Species  
**Group:** Elephantid mammal  

## Time

Mainly Pleistocene, with isolated populations surviving into the Holocene.

Roughly **400,000 years ago to about 4,000 years ago**.

## Ecology

**Diet:** Herbivore  
**Movement:** Quadrupedal  
**Habitat:** Cold steppe-tundra and mammoth-steppe environments  

Important traits:

- long insulating fur
- thick fat layer
- relatively small ears
- large curved tusks
- cold-adapted elephant-like body

## Main Locations

- Mammoth Steppe
- Beringia
- Siberia
- Alaska
- Wrangel Island

## Relationships

- **Humans:** genuinely coexisted in some regions
- **T. rex:** did not coexist
- **Triceratops:** did not coexist
- **Edmontosaurus:** did not coexist
- **Ankylosaurus:** did not coexist

## Interesting Fact

An isolated woolly mammoth population survived on **Wrangel Island until about 4,000 years ago**.

## Uncertainty

The exact causes of woolly mammoth extinction are still debated and likely involved a combination of climate change, habitat loss, and human pressure.
