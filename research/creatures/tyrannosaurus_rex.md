# Tyrannosaurus rex — Quick Research Notes

**Scientific name:** *Tyrannosaurus rex*  
**Rank:** Species  
**Group:** Tyrannosaurid theropod carnivore  

## Time

Late Cretaceous, about **68–66 Ma**.

## Ecology

**Diet:** Carnivore  
**Movement:** Bipedal  
**Size:** About **11–13 m** long  

Important traits:

- extremely powerful jaws
- bone-crushing bite
- large serrated teeth
- strong sense of smell
- active predator that also scavenged

## Main Locations

- Hell Creek Formation
- Western North America
- Montana, Wyoming, South Dakota, and nearby regions

## Relationships

- **Triceratops:** same ecosystem; possible predator-prey relationship
- **Edmontosaurus:** same ecosystem; strong predator-prey evidence
- **Ankylosaurus:** same ecosystem
- **Woolly mammoth:** did not coexist

## Interesting Fact

A T. rex tooth has been found embedded in a healed hadrosaur vertebra, showing that T. rex attacked living prey.

## Uncertainty

Exact body mass varies by specimen, pack hunting is uncertain, and not every bite mark proves active predation rather than scavenging.
