# Ankylosaurus — Quick Research Notes

**Scientific name:** *Ankylosaurus magniventris*  
**Rank:** Species  
**Group:** Armoured ankylosaurid herbivore  

## Time

Late Cretaceous, about **68–66 Ma**.

## Ecology

**Diet:** Herbivore  
**Movement:** Quadrupedal  
**Size:** About **6–8 m** long  

Important traits:

- heavily armoured body
- bony osteoderms
- broad, low-slung build
- large tail club
- low-browsing feeding style

## Main Locations

- Hell Creek Formation
- Lance Formation
- Western North America

## Relationships

- **T. rex:** same ecosystem; possible predator-defense interaction
- **Triceratops:** same ecosystem
- **Edmontosaurus:** same ecosystem
- **Woolly mammoth:** did not coexist

## Interesting Fact

Ankylosaurid tail clubs were capable of delivering powerful strikes and may have been used for defense or competition.

## Uncertainty

No complete Ankylosaurus skeleton has been discovered, so some details of its armour arrangement and exact appearance are still reconstructed.
