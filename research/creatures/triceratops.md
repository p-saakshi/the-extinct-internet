# Triceratops — Quick Research Notes

**Scientific name:** *Triceratops*  
**Rank:** Genus  
**Group:** Ceratopsid herbivore  

## Time

Late Cretaceous, about **68–66 Ma**.

Important species:

- *T. horridus*
- *T. prorsus*

## Ecology

**Diet:** Herbivore  
**Movement:** Quadrupedal  
**Size:** About **8–9 m** long  

Important traits:

- two large brow horns
- one nasal horn
- large bony frill
- keratinous beak
- dental batteries for processing vegetation

## Main Locations

- Hell Creek Formation
- Lance Formation
- Western North America

## Relationships

- **T. rex:** same ecosystem; possible predator-prey relationship
- **Edmontosaurus:** same ecosystem
- **Ankylosaurus:** same ecosystem
- **Woolly mammoth:** did not coexist

## Interesting Fact

Triceratops horns may have been used for defense, display, or competition between individuals.

## Uncertainty

Large permanent herds are not firmly established, and the exact functions of the horns and frill are still debated.
