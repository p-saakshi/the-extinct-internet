# Edmontosaurus — Quick Research Notes

**Scientific name:** *Edmontosaurus*  
**Rank:** Genus  
**Group:** Hadrosaurid herbivore  

## Time

Late Cretaceous, about **73–66 Ma**.

Important species:

- *E. regalis* — older, associated with the Horseshoe Canyon Formation
- *E. annectens* — later, associated with the Hell Creek Formation

## Ecology

**Diet:** Herbivore  
**Movement:** Could walk on **2 or 4 legs**  
**Size:** Up to about **12–13 m**

Important traits:

- broad beak
- large dental batteries
- hundreds of replacement teeth
- adapted for processing tough vegetation

## Main Locations

- Hell Creek Formation
- Horseshoe Canyon Formation
- Lance Formation
- Western North America

## Relationships

- **T. rex:** strong predator-prey evidence
- **Triceratops:** same ecosystem
- **Ankylosaurus:** same ecosystem
- **Woolly mammoth:** did not coexist

## Interesting Fact

Large Edmontosaurus bonebeds suggest they may have lived or travelled in groups.

## Uncertainty

Exact herd structure, body mass, and detailed diet are not fully known. Hell Creek interactions mainly apply to *E. annectens*.
